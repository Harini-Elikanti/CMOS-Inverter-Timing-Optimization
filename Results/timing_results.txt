CMOS INVERTER TIMING CHARACTERIZATION
=====================================

Technology: TSMC 180 nm
VDD: 1.8 V
Load capacitance: 1 pF
Temperature: 27 C
Simulator: LTspice

BASELINE DESIGN
---------------
NMOS: W = 900 nm, L = 180 nm
PMOS: W = 900 nm, L = 180 nm

tPHL       = 1.710 ns
tPLH       = 3.822 ns
Rise time  = 7.950 ns
Fall time  = 3.100 ns

Average propagation delay:
tp = (tPHL + tPLH)/2
   = 2.766 ns


PMOS SIZING OPTIMIZATION
------------------------
NMOS: W = 900 nm, L = 180 nm
PMOS: W = 1.8 um, L = 180 nm

tPHL       = 1.716 ns
tPLH       = 2.011 ns
Rise time  = 4.153 ns
Fall time  = 3.102 ns

Average propagation delay:
tp = (tPHL + tPLH)/2
   = 1.864 ns


COMPARISON
----------
PMOS width was increased from 900 nm to 1.8 um.

Average propagation delay:
2.766 ns -> 1.864 ns

Reduction:
approximately 32.6%

Low-to-high propagation delay:
3.822 ns -> 2.011 ns

Reduction:
approximately 47.4%

Rise time:
7.950 ns -> 4.153 ns

Reduction:
approximately 47.8%


CONCLUSION
----------
Increasing PMOS width improves pull-up drive strength,
resulting in faster output charging and significantly
improved low-to-high transition performance.
